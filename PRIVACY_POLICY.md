# Movvy — Privacy Policy

**Operated by:** Movvy Technologies Inc. ("Movvy", "we", "us", "our"), a corporation incorporated in the Province of Alberta, Canada.
**Effective Date:** July 5, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

Movvy respects your privacy and complies with Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (**PIPEDA**) and applicable Alberta privacy law. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use and protect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

By using the Movvy app or website (the "Services"), you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described here.

---

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

Movvy Technologies Inc. is the organization accountable for the personal information under its control. Our **Privacy Officer** can be reached at **management@movvy.ca** (see Section 15).

---

## 2. Information we collect

**a) Account & identity**
- Name, email address, phone number, city, password (stored only as a secure hash), and role (Customer, Mover, or Company).

**b) Partner/Crew verification (Movers and Companies only)**
- Business details, vehicle information, documents you upload (e.g., licences, insurance, identity), and background-check status where applicable.

**c) Move information**
- Pickup and drop-off addresses, dates and time windows, item lists, access notes, and messages you send to your Crew or to support.

**d) Location**
- Approximate location when you create a Booking. **Live GPS** is collected only during an active move and only if you grant permission; a Crew's device shares location while a move is in progress so the Customer can track it.

**e) Payment information**
- If and when you pay through the App, card details are handled by a third-party **PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor**. **Movvy does not collect or store full payment-card numbers.** We retain non-sensitive transaction records (amounts, dates, GST, payout references) for accounting.

**f) Communications**
- In-app chat messages, support conversations, and **metadata** for masked calls/SMS (such as time, duration, and the parties connected) processed through our telephony provider.

**g) Device & usage**
- App version, operating system, device identifiers, IP address, crash logs, and basic analytics (feature usage, page views) used to keep the Services reliable and secure.

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of the Services, and — for Crews — from verification providers you authorize.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:
- **Deliver the Services:** create your account, match Customers with Crews, calculate hourly pricing, enable tracking and masked communication, process payments, and send move-status updates.
- **Verify and keep the platform safe:** identity/eligibility checks, fraud prevention, rate limiting, audit logging, and enforcing our Terms.
- **Provide support:** answer questions and resolve disputes, including through an AI-assisted support assistant (Section 6).
- **Improve the product:** using aggregated or de-identified usage data to guide roadmap decisions.
- **Meet legal and tax obligations:** invoicing, GST/HST records, and responding to lawful requests.

We rely on your **consent** (which you may withdraw, subject to legal and contractual limits) and, where permitted, on grounds such as performing our contract with you, our legitimate business interests in operating a safe marketplace, and compliance with law.

---

## 4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to run the Services:

- **The Crew assigned to your move** — limited to what's needed to perform it: your name, pickup and drop-off details, access notes, and a masked phone connection (your real number is not shared).
- **Service providers who power Movvy**, bound by contract to process data only on our behalf:
  - **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage (primary data hosting, Canada Central region).
  - **Twilio** — SMS one-time passcodes and masked calling.
  - **Google Maps Platform** — geocoding, addresses, and routing/ETAs.
  - **Resend** — transactional email delivery.
  - **Anthropic** — the AI service that powers first-line support replies (Section 6).
  - **Expo / Vercel** — mobile delivery/push infrastructure and website hosting.
  - **A background-check provider** — only if and when Crew screening is enabled, and only for Crews.
  - **A payment processor** — only if and when in-app card payments are enabled.
- **Authorities or third parties** where required by law, court order, or valid law-enforcement request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or Movvy.
- **A successor entity** in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

**We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertising networks.**

---

## 5. Location data

Live location is used only to operate an active move: a Crew shares location so the Customer can track progress and ETA, and Customers' approximate location helps with booking. You can grant or revoke location permission in your device settings; revoking it will limit tracking features. We do not use your location for advertising.

---

## 6. AI-assisted support

Support chats may first be answered by an automated assistant that can respond to common questions and escalate to a human when needed. To do this, the content of your support messages and limited context about your move may be processed by our AI provider (**Anthropic**) and may be reviewed by Movvy staff for quality and resolution. This content is **not** used to train third-party models. Please don't include payment-card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information in support chat.

---

## 7. Where your information is stored (cross-border)

Primary data is stored in **Canada** (Supabase, Central region), and backups are encrypted and stored in Canadian cloud regions. Some service providers (for example, telephony, mapping, email, AI, push, and hosting) may process limited information on servers **outside Canada, including in the United States**. When information is processed in another country, it may be accessible to that country's authorities under its laws. We use providers that offer appropriate contractual and security protections.

---

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Active account data:** while your account exists.
- **Completed move records and invoices:** retained about **7 years** to meet Canadian tax and accounting requirements.
- **Audit and security logs:** about **1 year**, then purged.
- **Support and dispute records:** as long as needed to resolve issues and for a reasonable period afterward.

You can delete your account any time (Customer: **Profile → Delete account**). When you do, we remove or de-identify your personal information, except records we must keep for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons (for example, anonymized/necessary invoice records).

---

## 9. How we protect it

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including: row-level security on every database table, encryption in transit and at rest, secure credential storage on your device (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), rate limiting, access controls, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit access to those who need it.

---

## 10. Breach notification

If a breach of security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by PIPEDA, and we will keep records of breaches as the law requires.

---

## 11. Your rights (PIPEDA)

You may:
- **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** — update inaccurate information (much of it is editable in the in-app profile).
- **Withdraw consent / delete** — withdraw consent and delete your account at any time, subject to legal and contractual limits.
- **Complain** — if you believe your rights have been violated, contact our Privacy Officer, and you may also file a complaint with the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca).

We respond to verified requests within the timeframes required by law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

---

## 12. Children

The Services are intended for adults **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have, we will delete it.

---

## 13. Marketing & communications choices

Essential transactional messages (one-time passcodes, booking and move-status updates, receipts, security notices) are part of the Services and cannot be turned off while you hold an account. Any optional marketing messages include an unsubscribe method, and you can reply STOP to marketing SMS.

---

## 14. Cookies & analytics

The mobile app uses minimal local storage for session tokens. The movvy.ca website uses only essential cookies and does not use third-party advertising trackers.
